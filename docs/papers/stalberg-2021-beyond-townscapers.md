# Beyond Townscapers

> [!summary]
> An informal talk about experiments after Townscaper and the author's general
> approach to tile-based procedural generation: which parts to hand-author,
> which to generate, and how to keep a small set of tiles flexible enough to
> produce varied structures.

## Citation

Oskar Stålberg. 2021. Beyond Townscapers. Talk at Sweden Game Conference
(SGC) 2021. Video: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uxeo9c-PX-w>

Link only: a recorded talk.

## Why it matters here

Background for designing the tile vocabulary of the fill layer. It is the
most recent of Stålberg's talks on tile generation and the one most concerned
with method rather than a single game.
