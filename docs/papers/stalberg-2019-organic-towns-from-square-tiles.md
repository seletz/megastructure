# Organic Towns from Square Tiles

> [!summary]
> A talk on the tile system behind Townscaper, a toy in which clicking on
> water grows a small town. The town looks hand-built and irregular, yet it is
> made from cubic tiles chosen by a constraint solver fast enough to react to
> each click. The talk explains how the tiles are designed, how the grid is
> deformed so it does not look like a grid, and how corners rather than
> centres of cells carry the information.

## Why it matters here

Background for the look of the fill layer: a small vocabulary of box-shaped
tiles can read as organic, hand-made architecture if the tiles are designed
around how they meet. It also shows that interactive-speed tile solving on a
small region is realistic, which matches the per-sector budget in
[[RESEARCH_WFC]].
