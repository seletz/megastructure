# The Story of Townscaper

> [!summary]
> How Townscaper came to be: the earlier experiments it grew out of, the
> technical ideas that made it work (an irregular grid, tiles placed at grid
> corners, a WFC-style solver), and the design decisions that turned a
> technology demo into a relaxing toy.

## Citation

Oskar Stålberg. 2021. The Story of Townscaper. Talk at Konsoll 2021, Bergen.
Video (published 2022): <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5xrRTOikBBg>

Link only: a recorded talk.

## Why it matters here

Background on turning a tile solver into something that looks deliberate:
most of the effort goes into the tile set and its rules, not the solver. That
matches the project's plan to keep the solver small and invest in tileset
validation.
